Privacy Policy

Gora Taxi Partner (package com.taxi.call_taxi_partner) — operated by Gora Cabs.
Last updated: 21 October 2026

Gora Cabs operates a taxi booking platform. The Gora Taxi Partner app is the app used by drivers and cab operators (“partners”). This policy explains what the app collects, why, who it is shared with, and how it can be deleted. It applies to the Android app and to the backend service behind it.

Information we collect

DataWhy we collect it
Name, mobile number, email, city To create and verify the partner account, sign in by OTP, and show the partner to the members they do business with.
KYC documents — vehicle registration (RC), driving licence, Aadhaar To verify that a partner and their vehicle are real and licensed before they can take bookings.
Photos taken with the camera Profile and vehicle photos, document uploads, and the two photos (car front, driver in the car) captured when a trip starts, which protect both the partner and the customer in a dispute.
Device location (precise) See the dedicated section below.
Wallet and payment records To run the security-wallet balance, commissions, recharges and payouts.
Push notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging) To alert the partner when a booking matching their city and vehicle type is posted.
Basic device and app information, bookings, chat messages To operate the service, support the partner, and investigate fraud complaints.

How we use location data

The app asks for location permission only after showing an in-app explanation, and only when one of the three uses below applies. Location can be declined and the app still works.

  • Recording trip distance. Once the partner starts a trip, the app records the route driven and adds up the kilometres. This continues in the background and with the screen off, because the partner is driving, and an ongoing “Trip in progress” notification stays visible for the whole trip. The running kilometre total is what any extra-kilometre charge on the customer’s bill is calculated from. Only the kilometre total is sent to our servers — the route itself and the partner’s coordinates are never transmitted, stored or shared. Recording stops the moment the partner ends the trip.
  • Showing an approximate last-active area. A coarse area such as “Jaipur 302001, Rajasthan” is shown on the partner’s profile to members they connect with, so those members can trust who they are dealing with. Exact coordinates are never shown to anyone.
  • Placing a pin on the map. When the partner is choosing a pickup or drop point, the app can centre the map on their current position. This is used on the device only.

Location data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with third parties for their own purposes.

Sharing

  • With other members: a partner’s name, city, rating and — once a booking is confirmed — their contact number are shown to the member on the other side of that booking. Contact details are gated by membership to reduce spam and misuse.
  • With service providers we use to run the app: Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging for alerts, Maps and Directions for navigation), our payment gateway for recharges and payouts, our SMS provider for OTP, and our document-verification provider for RC, licence and Aadhaar checks. They process this data only to provide that service to us.
  • Where the law requires it, or to investigate fraud or abuse on the platform.

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

Retention and deletion

Account and booking records are kept for as long as the account is active, and afterwards only as long as needed for tax, accounting and dispute purposes.

A partner can request deletion of their account and data from inside the app (Settings → Delete Account), or by writing to the email below. On deletion we remove the profile, documents and contact details; records we are legally required to keep, such as completed-booking and payment history, are retained for the statutory period and then deleted.

Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored on access- controlled servers. Access is limited to staff who need it to operate the service or to handle a support or fraud case.

Children

The app is intended for commercial drivers and cab operators. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed, and continued use of the app after a change means the updated policy applies.
